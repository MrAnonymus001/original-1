About

“Every missed call is a client who called someone else.”

That’s Hamza in Faisalabad, Pakistan. He started out as a motion designer and video editor, and over 3+ years he completed 90+ projects for founders, coaches, startups and creators. He also ran a small team of vetted editors, so quality never depended on one person’s time.

But there was a problem he kept running into. When he was deep in a client’s edit, he couldn’t pick up the phone. By the time he called back, the lead had already moved on to another editor. Again and again, potential clients were lost, not because of the work, but because of a missed call.

So he decided to fix it. He started building something that would answer every call, at any hour, and never let a lead go cold.

That became Viewora. Today Viewora builds realistic AI calling agents that answer calls, follow up with leads and keep conversations going, for clinics, dentists, restaurants, roofing companies, law firms, plumbers and other local businesses.

Because a business shouldn’t lose a client just because the phone rang at the wrong time.

FAQ

Fair questions.

The things people ask on the first call. Anything else, just ask me.

Who actually builds my agent?

Our team. The same people who scope your agent on the first call build, test, and maintain it. No offshore handoff, no template dropped in and forgotten.

What if you're unavailable?

Your agent runs on Retell AI's cloud infrastructure, so it keeps answering around the clock, no matter what. You also get your own access to the platform, so you're never locked out of your agent.

Will callers know it's AI?

Some will, and most won't care. It speaks naturally, handles interruptions, and gets to the point. If someone wants a person, it transfers or takes a message, your call.

What can it connect to?

Calendars, CRMs, booking and POS systems, SMS, email, and most tools with an API. If your system has a way in, the agent can read from it and write to it mid call.

Can you handle patient calls?

Yes. We build HIPAA compliant voice agents for clinics and healthcare practices, so patient calls can be answered, routed, and booked without putting protected information at risk.

What languages does it speak?

Pretty much any language your callers speak. One agent can switch languages mid call if the caller does.
